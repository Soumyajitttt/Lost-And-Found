FAQs

Good questions. Clear answers.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Creating an account and posting lost or found items is free.

Who can use it?

Anyone in the community it's set up for, such as students and faculty on a campus. Register with your email to get started.

How does matching work?

The platform compares lost and found posts by category and description, then shows likely matches so you can follow up.

Why is my post pending?

New posts are reviewed by an admin before they appear on the board. Once verified, the status changes automatically.

What if I found something?

Post it as "Found" with a clear description and location. If it matches a lost item, the owner can reach out to you.
